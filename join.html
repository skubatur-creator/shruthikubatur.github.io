Join the group

I am building a new research group at UTEP and looking for people who want to work on how AI agents fail and how to make them reliable.

PhD students

I am recruiting PhD students to start in Spring/Fall 2027. Good preparation is a strong background in probability or statistics, comfort with Python and PyTorch, and real curiosity about why systems break. Experience with LLM agents, computer vision, or simulation is helpful but not required; I care more about how you think than what you have already used.

To apply, submit an application to the UTEP Computer Science PhD program and name me as a faculty member you would like to work with. Before or after applying, send me an email with your CV, a short note on what interests you about the group's work, and one thing you have built or studied that you are proud of. Put "PhD applicant" in the subject line so I can find it.

Masters and undergraduate students at UTEP

I am working with UTEP masters and undergraduate students now. Projects in the group are hands-on: running agents, collecting and labeling failure traces, building evaluation tools, and analyzing where things go wrong. If you are a current UTEP student and this sounds interesting, email me with your CV, the courses you have taken, and what you would like to work on. There is no minimum experience; I am happy to start with someone who is curious and reliable.

What the group is like

Small, focused, and collaborative. I spend time with students rather than managing them from a distance, and I expect the same investment back. Students learn to read and write papers, to run careful experiments, and to explain their work to people outside the field. My industry background means I also care that what we build is usable, and I will help you find internships and industry connections when the time is right.
